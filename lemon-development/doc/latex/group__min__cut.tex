Algorithms for finding minimum cut in graphs. 

This group contains the algorithms for finding minimum cut in graphs.

The {\itshape minimum} {\itshape cut} {\itshape problem} is to find a non-\/empty and non-\/complete $X$ subset of the nodes with minimum overall capacity on outgoing arcs. Formally, there is a $G=(V,A)$ digraph, a $cap: A\rightarrow\mathbf{R}^+_0$ capacity function. The minimum cut is the $X$ solution of the next optimization problem\+:

\[ \min_{X \subset V, X\not\in \{\emptyset, V\}} \sum_{uv\in A: u\in X, v\not\in X}cap(uv) \]

LEMON contains several algorithms related to minimum cut problems\+:


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Hao-\/\+Orlin algorithm for calculating minimum cut in directed graphs.
\item Nagamochi-\/\+Ibaraki algorithm for calculating minimum cut in undirected graphs.
\item Gomory-\/\+Hu tree computation for calculating all-\/pairs minimum cut in undirected graphs.
\end{DoxyItemize}
